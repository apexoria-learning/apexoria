# Backlink strategy (off-site playbook — no on-site code)

**Quadrant:** Q3 (Tough & High Impact)
**Priority within quadrant:** 8
**Status:** Not started
**Owner (proposed):** user (execution) + Orchestrator (tracking) — no engineering agent involvement
**Impact:** High (foundational — nothing else in SEO moves without this)
**Effort:** Medium (ongoing, 3–5 hrs/week for 3 months)
**Source audit item(s):** 2026-08-03 SEO diagnostic — Domain Rating 0, Page Rating 0, 0 referring domains

## Why

Apexoria's on-page SEO (Phase 1 + Phase 2A perf work) is now genuinely competitive: titles, meta, schema.org markup, and image alt attributes are all in place. But without external links pointing at `apexorialearning.in`, Google has no trust signal to rank the site for any competitive term. **A DR-0 site with perfect on-page SEO will still rank below a DR-30 competitor with mediocre on-page.**

This is an **off-site** task — no code changes, no PRs. It's a marketing playbook you execute manually. Track the checklist here so multiple sessions don't re-do work.

**Goal (Q4 2026 target):**
- Referring domains: 0 → 40
- Domain Rating (Ahrefs) or Authority Score (Semrush): 0 → 15–25
- At least 3 links from `.edu` / `.gov.in` / partner university domains
- Track weekly in Google Search Console → Links → External

## Anti-patterns — never do these

- ❌ **Buy PBN (private blog network) backlinks** — algorithmic penalty risk (Google Penguin). Sites offering "50 backlinks for $50" are always PBN.
- ❌ **Reciprocal link exchanges at scale** — "I'll link to you if you link to me" schemes trigger Google's link-scheme classifier.
- ❌ **Keyword-stuffed exact-match anchors** — never use "salesforce training and placement in India" as an anchor. Vary anchors per the ratio in Phase B.
- ❌ **Auto-submit to 1000 directories** (SEnuke, GSA SER, etc.) — algorithmic devaluation, may even trigger manual action penalty.
- ❌ **Guest posts on obviously PBN sites** — check if the site publishes 20 unrelated guest posts per day, has thin content, or DA is suspiciously high vs traffic. If it looks like a link farm, it is.
- ❌ **Comment spam** — dropping links in blog comments as guest / anonymous. Zero SEO value since 2013, and damages brand reputation.
- ❌ **Fiverr backlink gigs** — 99% are PBN or spam. The 1% that aren't cost 10× what Fiverr charges.
